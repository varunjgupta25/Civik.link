Our Story
From a College Project to a Live Civic Platform

civik.link began as a college project — but somewhere between writing the first line of code and watching the splash screen load on a real phone, it became something much bigger than an assignment.

Varun Gupta, a Computer Science engineering student at Mumbai's Shree L.R. Tiwari College of Engineering, built civik.link with a simple but powerful observation: India's elderly and differently-abled citizens are often the most entitled to government welfare, yet the least equipped to access it. Scheme portals are complex, medical records are scattered across paper slips, and in an emergency, precious seconds are lost fumbling through contacts.

civik.link was built to fix that. In just a few months of development — learning full-stack architecture, integrating Groq AI, and deploying to a live production server — Varun shipped a complete, tested, and accessible platform that puts AI-powered health monitoring, government scheme discovery, and one-tap emergency SOS into the hands of every citizen.

This is Version 1.0. The mission has just begun.
