1. Who We Are

civik.link ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is a civic technology platform designed to empower elderly citizens, differently-abled individuals, and underserved communities in India. We provide AI-powered health monitoring, access to government welfare schemes, and an Emergency SOS system.

This Privacy Policy applies to all services offered through https://civik-backend-nydn.onrender.com and any future domains associated with civik.link.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our services. We do not collect data that is not required for app functionality.

a) Account Data (Required)

  • Email address — used as your unique login identifier
  • A unique User ID (UDID) — auto-generated from your email, e.g. CVLK-A3F9-2B7E
  • Account creation timestamp

b) Profile Data (You provide voluntarily)

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Age, gender, state of residence
  • Income bracket (for scheme eligibility)

c) Health Data (You provide voluntarily)

  • Blood pressure, blood sugar, weight readings
  • Medications and health conditions
  • Doctor's notes entered by you

d) Emergency Contact Data (You provide voluntarily)

  • Name, phone number, and relationship of your emergency contacts

e) Technical Data (Automatically collected)

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Timestamp of login sessions
We do not collect passwords, payment information, biometric data, location coordinates, or government identity numbers (Aadhaar, PAN).

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the civik.link platform:

  • Authentication: Your email is used to send a One-Time Password (OTP) to verify your identity
  • Personalization: Your profile data is used to personalise your dashboard and government scheme recommendations
  • Health Monitoring: Your health readings are displayed back to you on your private dashboard
  • Emergency SOS: Your emergency contact information is used to pre-fill the WhatsApp SOS message when you activate it
  • AI Assistance: Your health and profile data is sent to our AI service (Groq) to generate personalised responses to your queries
  • Service Improvement: Aggregated, anonymised usage data may be used to improve the platform

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or use it for any purpose not listed above.

4. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

When you use the AI Assistant feature, your health context and chat messages are transmitted to Groq, Inc. (USA) to generate a response. This data is processed in real time and is not stored by Groq for training purposes under their standard API terms.

We recommend you do not share highly sensitive medical details (e.g. specific diagnoses or medications) in the AI chat if you are concerned about third-party processing.

OTP Delivery: When you log in, your email address is transmitted to Gmail's SMTP servers (Google LLC) solely to deliver your login verification code.

Emergency SOS: When you activate SOS, the app opens WhatsApp on your device. This action is performed locally on your phone — we do not transmit any data to WhatsApp servers ourselves.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a secure database on servers hosted by Render.com (USA). Render maintains industry-standard security certifications.

Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Your login sessions are secured using JWT (JSON Web Token) technology.

Access Control: Your health data and profile are only accessible with your valid session token. No employee or third party has routine access to your data.

Passwords: civik.link uses a passwordless OTP system. We never store passwords.

⚠️ Current Limitation: civik.link is currently in Version 1.0 running on a free hosting tier. Data backups are not yet fully automated. We recommend not storing irreplaceable medical records exclusively on this platform at this stage.

6. Data Sharing

We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Groq, Inc.: AI query processing (see Section 4)
  • Google LLC: OTP email delivery via Gmail SMTP
  • Render.com: Hosting and infrastructure provider
  • Legal Requirement: If required by applicable Indian law, court order, or government authority

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to any commercial third party, advertiser, or data broker.

7. Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and applicable privacy standards, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: You can update or correct your profile data directly within the app at any time
  • Right to Erasure: You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Right to Grievance: You can raise a complaint about our data practices
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for non-essential data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at civik.link.official@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

civik.link is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you request account deletion, we will erase all personally identifiable data within 30 days.

Anonymised, aggregated data (e.g. total number of users) may be retained indefinitely for platform analytics.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Email: civik.link.official@gmail.com
  • Platform: civik.link
  • Response Time: Within 30 business days
